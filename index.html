Common questions about Vinted, eBay and Depop tax

Short answers with the gov.uk source. Not advice — see the note at the bottom of the page.

Do I have to pay tax on Vinted, eBay, Depop or Etsy sales?

It depends on what you sell, not on which app you use. gov.uk says selling your own unwanted things from time to time is usually not trading, so there is usually nothing to tell HMRC, however much it adds up to. Buying or making things in order to sell them is trading: gov.uk says people whose gross trading income is over £1,000 in a tax year (6 April to 5 April) need to register for Self Assessment, and tax, if any, is worked out on the profit after costs. Only HMRC or an accountant can say which applies to you. HMRC: Tax help for hustles

What is the HMRC "30 item rule" on Vinted and eBay?

It is a reporting rule, not a tax rule. Since January 2024, platforms such as Vinted, eBay, Depop and Etsy have had to send HMRC the details of sellers who reach 30 sales of goods or about £1,700 (€2,000) on that platform in a calendar year. They report by 31 January and give the seller a copy. HMRC's own page says a platform report "does not automatically mean you owe tax". gov.uk: Selling goods or services on a digital platform

Vinted has asked for my National Insurance number. What is this HMRC form?

Once you cross the reporting line, the platform is required by law to collect and report your name, address, date of birth, National Insurance number and your total for the calendar year (less its fees). Filling it in is not declaring income and is not a tax return. Whether any tax is due depends on the separate trading-allowance question. Vinted: UK reporting

How much can I earn on Vinted before paying tax?

For things you bought or made to sell, the trading allowance is £1,000 of gross trading income per tax year (6 April to 5 April). At £1,000 or less, gov.uk says there is no need to tell HMRC but you must keep records. Over £1,000, gov.uk says to register for Self Assessment by 5 October after the tax year ends; tax, if any, is on the profit after deducting either your costs or the £1,000 allowance. From 6 April 2027 the allowance rises to £3,000 (Budget, 28 October 2026; not yet law). Your own old clothes and belongings do not use up the allowance. gov.uk: Tax-free allowances on property and trading income

Why doesn't the figure Vinted or eBay sends HMRC match my own total?

Platforms report the calendar year (1 January to 31 December) and deduct their fees; the trading allowance is measured over the tax year (6 April to 5 April) on gross income. The two will never line up, which is why it helps to keep your own log of every sale.
