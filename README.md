# Am I over the HMRC line?

A free, no-sign-up checker for UK sellers on Vinted, eBay, Depop and Etsy. Type your
numbers and see where you stand against the two lines that matter — the platform-reporting
line (30 sales / €2,000, per platform, per calendar year) and the £1,000 trading allowance
(per tax year) — with the gov.uk page each figure comes from.

Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere. It runs entirely in your browser.

Not tax, legal or financial advice: it adds up what you type and shows the published
thresholds, with links. Always check gov.uk or speak to HMRC or an accountant about your
own situation.
